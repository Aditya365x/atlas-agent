# Northwind Logistics: Customer FAQ

## What plans do you offer?

We offer three plans. Starter is free and includes up to 50 shipments a month with standard support by email. Growth costs 4,999 INR per month and includes 1,000 shipments, a 12 percent discount on shipping rates, and chat support. Enterprise is custom priced, with volume discounts, a dedicated account manager and a 99.95 percent uptime SLA for the API.

## Which cities do you cover?

Same-day delivery is available in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune. Next-day delivery covers more than 19,000 PIN codes across India. Economy delivery reaches every serviceable PIN code within 3 to 6 days.

## How fast is same-day delivery?

Parcels booked before 13:00 are delivered by 21:00 the same day. Our on-time rate for same-day delivery was 96.4 percent in the last quarter. If a same-day parcel arrives late you automatically receive a service credit.

## What can't I ship?

We do not carry hazardous goods such as gas cylinders, lithium batteries shipped on their own, firearms, cash, or live animals. Perishables are allowed only with the Cold Chain add-on, which keeps parcels between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius.

## Is my shipment insured?

Every shipment is covered up to 5,000 INR at no cost. You can buy extra cover up to 5 lakh INR for 1.2 percent of the declared value. Claims must be filed within 7 days of delivery, with photos of the damaged parcel and packaging.

## How do I return an item?

Create a return from the original shipment in the dashboard or through the API. Return pickups happen within 2 working days. Return shipping is charged at the same rate as the outbound service level.

## Do you offer cash on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available for parcels up to 50,000 INR. The COD fee is 1.5 percent of the collected amount with a minimum of 30 INR. Collected cash is transferred to your bank account twice a week, on Tuesdays and Fridays.

## How do I contact support?

Starter customers can email support@northwind.example and get a response within 24 hours. Growth customers also have chat support from 08:00 to 22:00 IST with a median first response under 3 minutes. Enterprise customers get a 24x7 phone line and a dedicated account manager.

## Where is my data stored?

All customer data is stored in data centres in Mumbai and Hyderabad. We are ISO 27001 certified and complete a SOC 2 Type II audit every year. Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or later.

## Can I integrate with my store?

We have plug-ins for Shopify, WooCommerce and Magento that sync orders automatically and print labels in bulk. For anything else use the Freight API, which has official SDKs for Python, Node.js and PHP.
